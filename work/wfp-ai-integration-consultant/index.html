About the work

Since July 2026, I have worked as an AI Integration Consultant in Investigative Technologies at the World Food Programme’s Office of Inspector General. My focus is on bringing AI, organisational knowledge and everyday workflows together in ways that people can understand and use.

I approach this work as an engineering task: understand what people need, connect the right tools and information, and check that the result is reliable enough for its intended use. This role follows my AI engineering internship at WFP from October 2025 to June 2026.

Connecting AI with the right information

A useful AI assistant needs more than a capable language model. It needs relevant information and a clear way to show where an answer came from. My work focuses on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): finding useful material, giving the model that context and keeping supporting references visible. Microsoft Foundry and Azure AI Search are central to my wider AI engineering work.

Making agents useful in everyday work

I focus on connecting AI agents with tools and workflows so they can help with a task, rather than only produce a reply. That means thinking through the steps involved, what information each step needs and where a person should make the final decision. The goal is to reduce repetitive effort while keeping the work understandable and reviewable.

Checking quality and protecting information

I pay attention to how an AI system behaves when information is missing, unclear or outside its scope. Evaluation, access controls and human review are part of that approach. An answer should be checked against its sources, and the system should make its limits clear instead of presenting uncertain output as fact.

Supporting reliable use over time

Building the first version is only part of the work. I also focus on clear documentation, testing, feedback and visibility into how a system is behaving. These help teams understand the tools, identify problems and make considered improvements as their needs change.

What this work involved

My aim is to make AI useful, understandable and responsible in a professional setting. This overview describes my areas of focus without sharing confidential projects, internal systems or case information.

Tools and methods: Microsoft Foundry, Azure AI Search, Agentic AI, Retrieval-augmented generation, AI evaluation, Security and governance.
