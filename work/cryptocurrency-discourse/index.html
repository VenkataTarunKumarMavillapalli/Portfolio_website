About the work

During this research internship, I studied cryptocurrency white papers using natural language processing and network analysis. The work looked at how ideas, entities and relationships appeared across a collection of 21 documents.

The research combined data science with computational social science. I helped refine research questions, interpret results and place the findings in a wider social and technical context.

Reading patterns across documents

I used named entity recognition to identify entities, topic modelling with Latent Dirichlet Allocation to explore themes, and dependency parsing to examine language structure. Sentiment analysis and semantic network analysis provided other ways to look at the same material.

Exploring connections

I used entity co-occurrence analysis to examine which entities appeared together. I also applied social network analysis, text classification and clustering to study groups and communication structures in the documents.

Interpreting the findings

I worked with the research team to extract and bring together topics, entities and relationships. The analysis was part of an iterative research process: refining the questions, examining the results and considering what those results could reasonably tell us.

What this work involved

I received a Certificate of Research Excellence and a recommendation for PhD programmes following this work.

Tools and methods: Natural language processing, Named entity recognition, Topic modelling, Network analysis, Text classification, Clustering.
