01 / The context

The question behind the work.

Explore how language representations and model choice affect classification of labelled real and fake news datasets.

02 / The contribution

What I worked on.

  1. 01

    Performed readability, co-occurrence, sentiment, named entity, and topic analysis.

  2. 02

    Compared TF-IDF and Word2Vec representations across conventional and neural models.

  3. 03

    Experimented with recurrent architectures and fine-tuned BERT.

  4. 04

    Presented an interactive Streamlit application with text analysis, predictions, and confidence visualisation.

03 / The outcome

What the work contributed.

An end-to-end academic workflow from text preparation and model comparison to an interactive interface. Dataset classification does not establish the truth of an arbitrary news article.
