About the work

My work at the World Food Programme brought together AI engineering, data preparation, reporting and automation. I worked within the Office of Inspections and Investigations, where handling sensitive information carefully was part of the job.

The tools needed to help people find information, prepare documents and follow operational work. I worked on systems that kept links to source material and included human review. Because the work is confidential, I describe the capabilities here without sharing case information.

Finding information with AI

I developed retrieval-augmented generation systems, often called RAG. These systems find relevant information in a collection before an AI model uses it to answer a question. I combined keyword, semantic and vector search with re-ranking and agent orchestration using Azure AI Search, Azure AI Foundry and GPT models.

Preparing the underlying data

I built pipelines to ingest, validate, index and create embeddings for more than 9,000 oversight records. I also supported the preparation of more than one million forensic records and messages through parsing, normalisation, validation, secure storage and indexing. Keeping source links and metadata was important for tracing an answer back to the records behind it.

Helping with documents

I built AI-assisted drafting and review workflows that accepted different document formats. They included citations, quality checks, structured exports and a human review step. The aim was to support the person reviewing the material while keeping the supporting information visible.

Making reporting easier

I created 39 Power BI dashboards for executive, chief-level and operational users. They covered workload, key performance indicators, case ageing, intake and regional trends. I also automated SharePoint-connected reporting pipelines, reaching approximately 95% daily refresh automation and reducing manual reporting work.

Connecting requests and approvals

Using Logic Apps, Power Automate and SharePoint Lists, I developed workflows for requests, approvals, notifications, status tracking and escalation. I also wrote technical documentation, governance materials, deployment notes and handover resources, and worked with international teams on planning, demos, feedback and testing.

What this work involved

This role combined building the tools with preparing the data, documenting decisions and helping colleagues use and maintain the systems.

Tools and methods: Azure AI Foundry, Azure AI Search, Azure OpenAI, Python, Power BI, Power Automate, Logic Apps, SharePoint.
