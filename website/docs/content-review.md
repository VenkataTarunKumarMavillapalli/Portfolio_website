## Scope and wording

- The collection currently has 22 work entries, 13 journey entries, and 12 credential/programme entries.
- Several work entries are thematic summaries within a professional role. They are not all separate deployed products.
- Course and capstone scenarios are clearly labelled as academic work. Microsoft solution-accelerator projects explicitly acknowledge replication/customisation.
- Capability and outcome descriptions are paraphrased from supplied sources; they are not independently verified deployment or performance claims.
- No private investigative case identifiers, evidence, internal service URLs, confidential screenshots, or credentials were copied from the OIGI source project.
- The market illustration on the Learning page is decorative and has no empirical data values.
- The portrait comes from page 1 of the supplied résumé. Its original resolution is 211×210 pixels; a higher-resolution portrait would improve the large-screen version.
