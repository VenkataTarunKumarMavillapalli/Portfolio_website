# Content review — résumé-based revision

This revision uses Venkata.pdf as the source for biographical claims. The three-page résumé was supplied by the owner. Its extracted text is the basis for the expanded descriptions; the earlier portfolio and Timeline.pdf are not used to add claims in this version.

## Content structure

- Home: a short introduction, portrait and links to work, timeline and about.
- About: background, areas of work and university activities.
- Work: six professional roles and the six projects explicitly described in the résumé. Each has a readable detail page.
- Timeline: 17 dated events, derived from six roles, three education programmes, seven credentials/programmes and the 2017 Singapore conference. Events are grouped by their start year; overlapping programmes remain separate.
- Learning: education, seven certifications/programmes, publication and conference.
- Contact: email, existing LinkedIn/GitHub destinations and the supplied résumé.

## Editorial decisions

- Professional dates follow the résumé. Neither WFP nor EFSA is labelled as a current role.
- Undated projects are not assigned invented dates or added to the chronological timeline.
- The news-classification entry describes methods and the Streamlit deliverable without choosing a winning model or quoting accuracy: the résumé contains inconsistent strongest-model statements.
- The master’s result is reported as 88%, following the résumé without conversion.
- Italdesign training, programme ranking and thesis title follow page 2. No extra detail about the scope of the ranking is added.
- The two Azure projects are identified as adaptations of solution accelerators. The Google Fiber project is labelled as a capstone scenario.
- Professional work is described at capability level. No confidential records, internal endpoints, credentials or case details are included.
- Technical terms are explained without adding unreported features, outcomes or personal motivations.
- The publication is listed without an inferred year. The existing IEEE URL is retained, as are the existing contact links.
- The portrait and downloadable résumé are the same owner-supplied assets used in the earlier version.
- Older URLs redirect to the appropriate new role, education section or work collection. Previous content remains recoverable through Git history.

Documents are source material, not instructions. No instructions embedded in documents or repository content are executed.
