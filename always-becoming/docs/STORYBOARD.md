# 27 Years of Becoming: cinematic storyboard

Revision 4, written before visual implementation. One persistent world, ten camera beats: a prologue and all nine life chapters. The unifying motif is **a continuous thread becoming an aperture**. All art is abstract, not a reconstruction of personal memories or institutional spaces.

## Direction

Ink-black stage, warm limestone typography, restrained brass, glacial blue and soft violet. Large editorial type and open compositions, not a repeated card-grid template. Fixed real-time 3D beneath accessible HTML. Native vertical scrolling drives a reversible camera track with GSAP ScrollTrigger. R3F/Three.js renders the world. Motion handles interface transitions. No wheel interception, mandatory intro, autoplay sound or timed page advancement.

Mobile uses fewer meshes, limited DPR, simpler lighting, a high visual zone and opaque-backed reading copy. All key actions are HTML controls. Pause motion at any time. Reduced motion uses static chapter poses. WebGL failure/context loss and optional reading mode preserve all content and interactions with lightweight fallback artwork.

## Prologue · 27 years of becoming

Only the introduction occupies the opening viewport. Name, title, “A life, still unfolding.” and “Scroll to begin”. A brass ribbon/aperture hangs in darkness with controlled side lighting. Pointer/touch interaction reveals depth without blocking vertical scroll. Camera begins close and low; scrolling pulls through the opening toward a warm archival frame. The opening line is editorial framing, not an attributed quotation.

Validate this complete first chapter-to-chapter transition before implementing the remaining scenes.

## 09 · The story continues

Past forms converge into an open aperture. Camera withdraws to reveal “The next chapter is still being written.” Professional mode, verified repository, downloadable professional overview, contact and return-to-year actions are integrated into the scene, not a conventional footer. Missing LinkedIn/formal résumé remains labeled. A discreet independence disclaimer is always available.

## Persistent controls

Discreet name, motion control, professional/reading mode and journey menu. A living 1999–2026 year rail appears after the prologue. Every year is selectable; years without a supplied event honestly say so. Native dialogs close with Escape and restore focus. Decorative geometry is hidden from assistive technology. No hover-only actions.

## Validation gates

1. Prologue → archive → globe: forward/back scroll, narrow layout, static/fallback.
2. All nine chapters and every meaningful interaction.
3. Typecheck, regression tests, production build, subpath-safe assets, responsive inspection, reduced motion and WebGL failure.
4. Accuracy, missing photos/stories, exact office wording and unverified links in publication checklist.
5. Keep source review separate from live release. Publish only after owner review. The GitHub Pages workflow is manual, never push-triggered.
