## Map data

The globe uses Natural Earth low-resolution geography, which Natural Earth releases into the public domain. The bundled coastlines were dissolved and simplified from the available Natural Earth low-resolution dataset; land dots were sampled from those polygons. National borders are not drawn. The map is a decorative biographical visual, not a statement about territorial claims.

Approximate city-centre coordinates represent Hyderabad, London, New York, Philadelphia, Naples, Parma and Rome. Curves show associations within the biography, not exact routes or a verified travel sequence.
