About me

Data Science and AI professional.

Professional background

I am a Data Science and AI professional with international experience at the World Food Programme (WFP), a United Nations agency, and the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), a European Union agency.

I hold a master’s degree in Data Science and a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science. My academic, professional and international experiences have taken me through London, New York, Delhi, Naples (Napoli), Parma, Rome and Singapore.

I have worked with global teams on data and AI projects at WFP and gained hands-on experience in data engineering at EFSA. I am a proud graduate of the Apple Developer Academy in Europe and a Microsoft Certified Azure AI Engineer.

Work and approach

I am passionate about using data, AI and cloud technologies to solve business challenges, deliver measurable results and support evidence-based decision-making. I believe in building solutions that improve efficiency, transparency and impact for teams.

My AI engineering work includes systems that find information in documents, connect models to tools, and help people draft or review material. Much of this work uses Azure services, with attention to source references, access controls, monitoring and human review.

My data work includes dashboards, validation rules and pipelines that help teams check information and understand their operations. I have also automated reporting refreshes, ticket updates and approval workflows, alongside writing documentation, testing changes and preparing handovers.

I bring together innovation, collaboration and purpose, with the aim of creating meaningful change that makes a difference.
