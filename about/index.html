About me

I’m Venkata Tarun Kumar Mavillapalli, an AI engineer and data professional.

My background

My background combines computer science, data science and digital product development. I studied computer science through a three-continental programme in India, the United Kingdom and the United States, then completed a master’s in Data Science at the University of Naples Federico II in Italy.

I also spent nine months at the Apple Developer Academy, working in international teams on iOS apps, design and product development. That experience connected the technical work with questions about who a product is for, how it works for them and how an idea can become something usable.

My professional experience includes the World Food Programme, the European Food Safety Authority, academic research, NeuraFlash and the A.J. Drexel Autism Institute. Across these roles, I’ve worked on AI systems, data quality, dashboards, automation and software development.

What I work on

In AI engineering, I’ve built systems that find information in documents, connect models to tools and help people draft or review material. Much of this work uses Azure services, with attention to source references, access controls, monitoring and human review.

In data work, I’ve built dashboards, validation rules and pipelines that help teams check information and understand their operations. I’ve also automated repetitive tasks such as reporting refreshes, ticket updates and approval workflows.

My roles have included writing documentation, working through feedback with colleagues, testing changes and preparing handovers. Those activities help the work remain understandable and usable after the initial implementation.

Outside my professional work
